# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The fundraising committee uses one shared Marketing Gmail account. Each person selects their committee name so searches, company ownership, emails and status updates are attributed correctly. Directors and administrators oversee the entire team.

## Product Purpose

The CRM keeps company research, duplicate checking, ownership, outreach, follow-ups, scheduled email, replies and sponsorship outcomes in one operational record. Success means members can approach companies without clashes and directors can understand team progress without checking separate files.

## Positioning

One Google Apps Script workspace joins a structured company database to the shared Gmail workflow while preserving per-member ownership and signatures.

## Operating Context

Members work from the same normal Gmail account, often prepare emails at night, send personalized batches and use a Google Sheet as the database. The old Excel workbook remains a historical reference and is not automatically imported.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Members can send and schedule their own assigned company emails.
- Company matching uses names, aliases, email, domain, phone, website and social account details.
- Administrators and directors can view all companies and member progress.
- Incoming Gmail messages must separate human replies from delivery failures, full mailboxes, out-of-office notices and automatic acknowledgements.
- Gmail and Apps Script provider limits still apply.
- Access is restricted to the shared Marketing Google account. Committee identity selection does not use a PIN and does not expire in the background.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is Company CRM for 全辩 Marketing. The uploaded official logo must be displayed without cropping or overlapping text. The interface uses a black, white and grey monochrome system.

## Evidence on Hand

The existing Apps Script implementation, Google Sheet schema, uploaded CRM logo, visual email studio and previous Excel database define the real workflow. No performance targets or sponsorship claims should be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- One source of truth for every active company approach.
- Ownership and progress remain visible across the committee.
- Automated email noise never masquerades as a human reply.
- Important actions are clear without requiring coding knowledge.
- Existing data survives safe upgrades.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The web interface must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast and responsive layouts for desktop and mobile browsers.
